Reactive Resume on a phone: two ways to hold the page

Three anchor screens, Documents, Builder Write and Builder Page, drawn twice. Direction A puts the page first and writes in a sheet over it. Direction B keeps today's phone web layout, with the outline first and the page one tab away. Everything is clickable: tabs, tag chips, cards (press and hold to select), sheet grabbers, section menus, lines on the page and the zoom bar.
