Builder Write and Page, and the cover letter editor

Direction B, “Outline first”, chosen by the owner at G2a. Phone builder: bottom tabs Write · Page · Design · Check, the outline as the hero, entries full screen, a tap on the page selects an entry and offers Edit entry. No undo or redo controls on phone: destructive edits show an Undo toast. Design, Check, Share & export, History and the Assistant are drawn in design.html; Documents and the app shell in app.html.

Every frame has a state switch where it has states, and interaction notes underneath. The index on the left lists every screen and its states; tablet screens appear when Device is Tablet. Interactive elements are at least 44 pt or extend their hit area to 44 pt (noted in the CSS where a visual is smaller).

DS3b · Desk & Paper tokens from packages/ui/src/styles/globals.css (same block as DS1) · Phosphor regular and fill icons, as in packages/ui/src/components/icon.tsx · page 1 is the Onyx template preview, later pages are typeset from packages/schema/src/resume/sample.ts · copy is from the web components named in the brief · keyboards, status bars and print dialogs are system UI drawn with neutral tokens.
