Reactive Resume on phones and tablets: app shell, documents, account and utilities

DS3a, in the owner's Direction B (“Outline first”). Every screen and state for the shell, sign-in, Documents, New document and import, Search, Settings, the public resume viewer and the ATS checker. Each frame has an id (used by reports/DS3a.md) and interaction notes. Colours, type, spacing, radii, shadows and motion are the tokens in tokens.md. Builder, Design and Career screens are in builder.html, design.html and career.html.

DS3a · Desk & Paper tokens from packages/ui/src/styles/globals.css, mapped for React Native in tokens.md · icons are Phosphor regular and fill, as in packages/ui/src/components/icon.tsx · page images are the template previews from apps/web/public/templates/jpg · people and companies are the glossary's fictional names.
