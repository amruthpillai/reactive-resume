Eight findings, ordered by impact

P11. Large text overwhelms the layoutReproducedDetails ↕

Documents breaks mid-word at Accessibility Large. At the largest size, title, error banner and filters occupy the viewport; navigation labels truncate.

Change: Reflow headers and settings values, keep secondary chrome scrollable, preserve readable navigation without disabling scaling.

P12. Dismissal can erase unfinished formsSource confirmedDetails ↕

Job and interview sheets store edits locally, then unmount on swipe/backdrop dismissal without a dirty-form guard.

Change: Reuse the existing Career discard guard; retain form state until explicit Discard.

P13. Unsynced drafts lack an offline entry pointSource confirmedDetails ↕

Cold launch opens an API-only library. Stored pending documents need a known ID to recover; they are not offered in Documents.

Change: List current account’s pending local documents as “Unsynced on this device.” Stored text is not established lost.

P24. Save job sits below optional detailSource confirmedDetails ↕

A quick Company/Role capture requires passing many optional fields and AI controls to reach Save.

Change: Pin Save; reveal optional fields under More details.

P25. Share PDF takes a configuration detourSource confirmedDetails ↕

Share starts on Link; Download lists five formats before its action.

Change: Offer immediate Share PDF with existing filename; tuck other formats into a selector.

P26. Stage “tabs” silently edit recordsSource confirmedDetails ↕

Tapping progress changes stage and offers no Undo; tab semantics imply navigation.

Change: Use clear Change stage action, or explicit edit semantics plus Undo.

P27. Next action lacks stage-specific focusSource confirmedDetails ↕

Saved jobs offer Schedule interview and Open workspace alongside preparation actions.

Change: Prioritize prepare application, interview preparation, or follow-up according to current state.

P28. Dates require ISO typingSource confirmedDetails ↕

Job application/follow-up fields ask for YYYY-MM-DD even though a native date field exists.

Change: Reuse native date picker; preserve optional clearing and timezone semantics.
